- New: drag your own character with the mouse. Drop it on a chair, sofa or beanbag to sit down, or anywhere on the floor to stand there.
- New: walking into a chair, sofa or beanbag with WASD sits you down. Press a direction key again to get up.
- Fixed: while editing the office, the editor panel no longer hides the bottom of the map, so you can place decorations there too.

- 新增：用 WASD 走進椅子、沙發或懶骨頭會自動坐下，再按一次方向鍵就會起身。
